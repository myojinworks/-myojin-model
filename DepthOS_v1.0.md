# DepthOS v1.0 → v1.1 Transition Boundary

## Status Classification and Forward-Preservation Rules

**Project:** MyojinModel / DepthOS  
**Author:** Myojin  
**Document Type:** Transition Boundary  
**Status:** Frozen Candidate  
**Purpose:** Prevent backward contamination from current research into historical v1.0

---

## 0. Boundary Purpose

This boundary preserves **DepthOS Technical Specification v1.0** as a **Historical / Early Architecture**, while preventing structures exposed in later research from being retroactively inserted into v1.0.

Current research includes structures such as:

- non-saturated reasoning
- U→C structures
- depth mobility
- premature closure detection
- multi-layer indeterminacy fields

Their relationship to DepthOS v1.0 remains subject to separate specification.

This document is intended as an **Audit Classification**, not a **Revision Decision**.

Therefore:

> **Audit Finding ≠ Revision Decision**

## 2. Boundary Principles

### 2.1 Audit Finding ≠ Revision Decision

An audit result is an observation about the current specification.

It does not directly determine how a later specification must be written.

Therefore:

> **Audit Finding ≠ Revision Decision**

A detected ambiguity may lead to clarification, operationalization, restriction, preservation, removal, or continued suspension.

No particular revision path is implied by the audit itself.

---

### 2.2 Historical Specification Must Not Be Retroactively Rewritten

DepthOS v1.0 represents an early architectural externalization of the research.

Later structures should not be inserted retroactively into v1.0 merely because they appear compatible with current research.

Therefore:

> **Historical Specification ≠ Current Research Position**

The historical document should remain available as a record of what had been explicitly externalized at that stage.

---

### 2.3 v1.1 May Require Reconstruction Rather Than Direct Extension

DepthOS v1.1 is not required to be a linear extension of v1.0.

It may require reconstruction from a different structural position after the unresolved boundaries have been examined.

However:

> **Historical Preservation ≠ Mandatory Architectural Discontinuity**

The existence of later research does not, by itself, establish that v1.1 must belong to a different structural layer or architecture.

That question remains open.

---

## 3. Required Boundaries Before v1.1

The following boundaries remain unresolved and should be examined before generation of a v1.1 technical specification.

### 3.1 Status Boundary

Each structural element should be distinguishable according to status categories such as:

- **Defined**
- **Named**
- **Hypothesized**
- **Operationalized**
- **Empirically Unverified**
- **Ambiguous**
- **Provisional**

These statuses must not be silently promoted into one another.

For example:

> **Model Definition ≠ Empirical Human Property**

and:

> **Named Component ≠ Operationalized Mechanism**

---

### 3.2 Operationalization Boundary

The following structures require further operational specification before they can function as implementation-level components:

- `depth_score`
- compatibility criterion
- premise activation control
- safe indeterminacy
- cognitive load threshold
- depth induction semantics
- pipeline mapping

At minimum, future operationalization may need to distinguish:

> **Input Type → Operation → Output Type**

and, where applicable:

> **Trigger → Intervention → Exit Condition**

The present boundary document does not define those mechanisms.

---

### 3.3 Directional Boundary

The meaning of:

> **Human → AGI/ASI**

remains ambiguous in v1.0.

It is not yet determined whether this denotes:

- transformation of human input into an AI-compatible representation,
- human-side cognitive preprocessing before AI interaction,
- bidirectional compatibility mediation,
- or another structure.

The directionality should therefore be re-examined before being carried forward.

---

### 3.4 IP Boundary

The provisional MIT License in v1.0 should not be treated as automatically applying to every layer of the research.

Future specification should distinguish, where relevant:

- documentation
- reference implementation
- software code
- underlying methods
- patent rights
- licensing rights

Therefore:

> **Document License ≠ Method License ≠ Patent Grant**

The precise IP boundary remains unresolved.

---

## 4. Current Research Position and Its Relation to v1.0

Later research has exposed structures including:

- non-saturated reasoning
- U→C structures
- depth mobility
- premature closure detection
- multi-layer indeterminacy fields

At present, these structures are observed **outside the explicit scope of DepthOS v1.0**.

They should therefore not be retroactively inserted into v1.0 without separate specification.

This does **not** establish that they belong to a different structural layer.

Therefore:

> **Outside Explicit v1.0 Scope ≠ Different Structural Layer**

Their relationship to v1.0 remains open.

---

## 5. Forward-Preservation Rules

The transition from v1.0 toward any later specification should preserve the following distinctions.

### 5.1 Definition and Evidence

> **Defined ≠ Empirically Established**

A structure may be formally defined without being empirically validated.

---

### 5.2 Hypothesis and Operationalization

> **Hypothesized ≠ Operationalized**

A research hypothesis does not become an implementation mechanism merely by receiving a technical name.

---

### 5.3 Structural Similarity and Identity

> **Structural Similarity ≠ Structural Identity**

A later research structure may resemble a v1.0 component without being identical to it.

---

### 5.4 Structural Recurrence and Generative Principle

> **Structural Recurrence ≠ Common Generative Principle**

Repeated appearance of similar structures across DepthOS, U→C, MCROS, or related research does not by itself establish a common generative origin.

---

### 5.5 Historical Continuity and Revision

> **Historical Continuity ≠ Requirement to Preserve Every Historical Assumption**

Preserving v1.0 as a historical specification does not require later versions to inherit every v1.0 assumption.

---

## 6. What This Boundary Does Not Establish

This document does not establish:

- a DepthOS v1.1 architecture
- a new depth scale
- a compatibility criterion
- an operational definition of `depth_score`
- an implementation of Depth Induction
- an empirical validation of Depth 2.0–5.0
- AGI/ASI properties
- U→C as an established AI mechanism
- a relationship between U→C and Indeterminacy Holding
- a shared generative principle across related research
- an implementation-ready safety system
- a final licensing model
- a requirement that v1.1 must be produced

These remain outside the present boundary.
